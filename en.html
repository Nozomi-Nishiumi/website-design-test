Staging area for comparing design variants of the Laboratory of Animal Behavioural Strategies website. Each card is a snapshot of one development branch. The sites themselves are written in Japanese.

#1 No JavaScript — enhanced (combines #2 and #4)

Branch: noscript-enhanced @ 59c759b · updated 2026-08-04
Keeps the lightness of the no-JavaScript build while reproducing, with a minimal amount of JavaScript, the seamless transition from the hero tile zoom into the section 1 background, plus the missile and 360° panorama effects. Missile control is always active (once the aim hits its vertical limit, the gesture hands over to page scrolling).

#2 No JavaScript — simple

Branch: noscript-base @ d2148ad · updated 2026-07-11
A static build that uses no JavaScript at all. No elaborate scroll effects; even the navigation menu opens and closes with CSS alone.
